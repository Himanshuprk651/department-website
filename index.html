About the Department

The Department of Economics at Sri Venkateswara College offers undergraduate teaching that balances rigorous theory with real-world application. Students learn through lectures, tutorials, data labs and research projects, and graduate ready for postgraduate study, civil services, finance and policy careers.

  • Small tutorial groups with regular feedback
  • Data analysis and econometrics practice
  • Student-run society that organises Blisspoint every year
  • Guest lectures by economists, bankers and policymakers

Academic Activities

Learning at the department continues outside the classroom through seminars, workshops and our annual fest.

Blisspoint: Annual Event

Blisspoint takes its name from the economist's point of maximum satisfaction. It is the department's annual event where students apply what they study to competitions, discussions and live simulations.

Event Format Team size
Policy Brief Challenge Case competition 3 members
Econ Quiz Quiz, prelims and finals 2 members
Market Simulation Live trading game Individual
Parliamentary Debate Debate on economic policy 2 members
